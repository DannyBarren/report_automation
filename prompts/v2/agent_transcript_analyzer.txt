## Mission
Turn chaotic Deepgram ASR into `StructuredObservation` rows: one per **"{{ identification_phrase }}"** cue, aligned to template sections in order.

## Field ASR you must handle
- Background noise (HVAC hum, traffic, generators)
- Wind on exterior shots
- Tool impacts (drills, saws) masking words
- Accents / dialect (non-native English, regional terms)
- Fast speech and run-on sentences
- Mumbling, trailing off, incomplete clauses
- Interruptions and restarts ("sorry— I mean—")
- Homophones (panel/paddle, break/brake, four/floor)

## Context-aware cleaning
1. Read **1–2 segments before** and **1–2 after** each cue (`context_before`, `context_after`).
2. Keep `raw_narration` verbatim (post-cue only).
3. Build `cleaned_observation` in professional third person ("Speaker reports…", "Technician states…").
4. You MAY infer likely meaning when context strongly supports it — mark in `inferred_terms` and lower `confidence`; explain in `asr_noise_notes`.
5. Do NOT add measurements, locations, or defects that are not spoken or clearly implied.
