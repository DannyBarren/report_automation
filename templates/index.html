How this demo works

GenerSwift turns a short guided recording into a structured PDF: templates in reports/*.json define sections; you speak “Mark this.” at each stop; the pipeline transcribes, aligns timestamps, extracts still frames, and renders a print-ready report.

1

Pick templates

Choose one or more report types below. Everything downstream — recorder script, Crew payload, PDF layout — reads from the same JSON.

2

Record on device

Use your phone on Wi‑Fi/LAN (or ngrok). For each section: hold steady, say “Mark this.”, then describe what you’re showing.

3

Generate PDF

Upload lands on the preview page. Run the pipeline, watch progress, then download the PDF with frames and narratives.
